When Semrush wins

  • You wear every hat. Semrush handles keywords, competitors, technical audits, content, and local SEO — no bolt-on tools needed.
  • You run an agency or serve small businesses. Its white-label reports and client templates save hours.
  • You care about content marketing. SEO Content Template tells you what to write and how to structure it.
  • You need local SEO. Ahrefs simply doesn't do local rank tracking and listing management.

When Ahrefs wins

  • Link building is the job. Ahrefs' backlink index is the largest and most up-to-date; its Site Explorer is the gold standard for link research.
  • You analyze competitor backlinks daily. If 80% of your work is "who links to them and can I get that link," Ahrefs is faster and deeper.
  • You want a cheaper entry for small projects. Ahrefs' Lite plan undercuts Semrush's Pro price for a single small site.

Frequently asked questions

Is Semrush or Ahrefs better?

Semrush is the better all-round tool because it includes keyword research, competitor analysis, site audits, content marketing, and local SEO in one subscription. Ahrefs has a stronger backlink index, so it is better if link building and backlink analysis are your main focus.

Which is cheaper, Semrush or Ahrefs?

Semrush Pro costs $129.95 per month and Ahrefs' standard plan costs $129 per month, so entry prices are nearly identical. Semrush includes more features at that price, while Ahrefs offers a cheaper Lite plan for smaller projects.

Does Ahrefs have better backlink data than Semrush?

Yes. Ahrefs maintains the largest and freshest backlink index, which makes it the stronger choice for detailed link building and link analysis. Semrush's backlink data is good but slightly smaller and less fresh.

Is Semrush better for local SEO than Ahrefs?

Yes. Semrush includes dedicated local SEO tools such as local rank tracking and listing management, which Ahrefs does not offer.

Can I try Semrush and Ahrefs before paying?

Both tools offer free trials. Semrush provides a 14-day trial and a limited free account, and Ahrefs offers a trial for new users. You can also use Ahrefs Webmaster Tools for free with limited data.

Which tool should a beginner choose: Semrush or Ahrefs?

A beginner should choose Semrush because its guided interface, free Academy courses, and broader feature set make the all-in-one SEO workflow easier to learn than Ahrefs.
