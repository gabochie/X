Frequently asked questions

What is the best free SEO tool?

Google Search Console is the best free SEO tool because it shows your real keyword rankings, clicks, impressions, and indexing issues straight from Google's data. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is the best free option for backlink data.

What is the cheapest SEO tool that still works well?

Ubersuggest is the cheapest paid SEO tool at about $12 per month, and it covers keyword research and basic audits. Semrush Pro at $129.95 per month is the cheapest complete all-in-one tool.

Is there a free version of Semrush?

Yes. Semrush offers a limited free account and a 14-day free trial of Pro. The free account includes limited keyword research and site audit credits.

Can I do SEO with only free tools?

Yes, you can do basic SEO with only free tools. Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Keyword Planner, and Ahrefs Webmaster Tools cover keywords, rankings, backlinks, and technical checks. Paid tools add speed and depth.

Is Ubersuggest good for SEO?

Ubersuggest is good for beginners on a tight budget because it combines keyword research, site audits, and rank tracking for about $12 per month, but its data is shallower than Semrush or Ahrefs.

What is the best budget alternative to Ahrefs?

Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is the best free budget alternative to paid Ahrefs, covering backlinks and site health for free. For a paid all-in-one alternative, Semrush Pro is the strongest budget value at $129.95 per month.
